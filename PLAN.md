# Follow-up decisions

- Observe five to eight administrative staff finding a known law collection,
  identifying the responsible school municipality and locating another municipality's
  example regulation. Use the recurring gaps to choose one extension below.
- Municipality-to-school relationships need a verified source and explicit records,
  including shared organisations; names alone do not establish coverage.
- A small document-link pilot is feasible: Adliswil, Uster, Zürich and Winterthur
  expose official document pages. Prefer at most two extra columns, `Gemeindeordnung`
  and `Personalrecht`, with no visible source or review-date fields. Choose the pilot
  municipalities before adding links; fees and organisation documents vary more by body.
- Search across document titles depends on that curated pilot. Full-text search,
  change notifications and an AI assistant remain deferred pending evidence of need.
